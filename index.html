FAQs

Helpful answers before you join.

Who are these courses for?

They are designed for beginners and curious learners who want practical, mentor-supported skills in design, trading, and code.

Do I need prior experience?

No. Each class is structured to help new learners build confidence and learn through real projects, feedback, and guided practice.

How do I join a cohort?

Complete the registration form above, share your details, and our team will send the next cohort information to your WhatsApp or inbox.

Are classes live and interactive?

Yes. The sessions are designed to be practical, interactive, and supportive so learners can ask questions and improve in real time.

What happens after I register?

After registration, you will receive updates on the next available session, onboarding details, and the best next step for your chosen track.
